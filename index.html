Daily Nova Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-28

Daily Nova does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Everything you create stays only on your phone. There is no server and no account, and I cannot see your data.

What stays on your phone

Your notes, journal entries, diary, tasks, habits, tags, photos, and quotes are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. The app does not use Artificial Intelligence.

Location

The app does not track your location. A location is saved on your device only when you choose to add it to a task, and it is never sent anywhere.

Microphone

You can use voice input instead of typing. Voice input may use your phone's built-in speech-to-text from Google. I do not record or keep your audio.

Fingerprint and PIN lock

These are handled by your phone. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint.

Sharing and saving

Notes and quotes leave your phone only when you choose to share or save them yourself.

Ads

The only outside service is Google AdMob, which shows one ad before a new quote is generated. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, and device information under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. I do not receive this information. You can reset or turn off ad personalization in your phone's Google settings.

Data retention and deletion

I hold none of your data. You can delete any note, task, habit, or photo inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because I do not collect your data, there is nothing for me to access, correct, or delete. If you have a privacy request or question, you can still email me.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
